Hunza Escape

A serene journey through the majestic Karakoram, blending rugged adventure with refined luxury accommodations.

Your Journey

DAY 1

Arrival in Islamabad & Onward to Chilas

Meet your guide at Islamabad International Airport. We depart immediately via the Karakoram Highway, witnessing the dramatic transition from plains to mountains. Evening arrival at our riverside lodge.

directions_car 10 hrs drive hotel Riverside Lodge

DAY 2

The Gates of Hunza

Journey further north, stopping at the junction of the world's three highest mountain ranges. Arrive in Karimabad, Hunza by afternoon. Check into your luxury cliffside resort.

DAY 3

Baltit Fort & Eagle's Nest

Morning exploration of the 700-year-old Baltit Fort. Late afternoon jeep ride to Duikar for a breathtaking sunset over the Rakaposhi peak.
